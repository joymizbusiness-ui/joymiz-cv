Help & FAQ

Getting Started

How do I create my CV?

Simply click "Builder" in the navigation or scroll down to the CV builder. Fill in your details across the five steps: Personal Information, Experience, Education, Skills, and Preview. Your CV updates in real-time as you type.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Joymiz CV requires no sign-up, no email, and no password. Just start building your CV immediately.

Is it really free?

Yes, completely free. There are no premium tiers, no hidden fees, and no "download for $X" paywalls. Every feature is free.

Using the Builder

Can I go back and edit previous steps?

Yes. Use the "Back" button or click any step in the step indicator at the top to navigate between sections. Your data is preserved as you move between steps.

How do I add multiple jobs or education entries?

Click the "+ Add Experience" or "+ Add Education" button to add additional entries. You can remove entries by clicking the "Remove" button on any entry.

How do I add skills?

Type a skill in the Skills input field and press Enter or type a comma. To remove a skill, click the × on the skill tag or press Backspace when the input is empty.

Can I change the CV template?

Yes. Use the template dropdown above the preview panel to switch between Classic, Modern, and Minimal templates. The preview updates instantly.

Downloading

How do I download my CV as a PDF?

Click the "Download PDF" button. This opens your browser's print dialog. Select "Save as PDF" as the destination and click Save. The print CSS ensures only the CV content is included.

Why does it use the browser print dialog?

We use window.print() with custom print CSS because it's the most reliable, free way to generate PDFs without requiring server-side processing or third-party libraries. It works on all modern browsers.

Which browsers are supported?

Joymiz CV works on all modern browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and their mobile versions.

Privacy & Data

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. All data you enter stays in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server. We have no database, no analytics tracking your CV data, and no way to see what you enter. When you close the page, your data is gone unless you saved the PDF.

Do you use cookies?

No. Joymiz CV does not use cookies or any tracking technologies.

Can I save my CV to come back later?

Currently, your data is only stored in your browser's memory. We recommend downloading your CV as a PDF when you're finished. We may add a local save feature in the future.

Tips for a Great CV

  • Keep it concise - Aim for 1-2 pages. Recruiters spend about 6 seconds on an initial CV scan.
  • Use action verbs - Start bullet points with words like "Led", "Developed", "Implemented", "Achieved".
  • Quantify results - Numbers stand out. "Increased sales by 25%" is stronger than "Increased sales".
  • Tailor each application - Adjust your summary and skills to match the job description.
  • Proofread carefully - Typos and grammar errors create a bad first impression.
  • Use a clean format - Our templates are designed to be ATS-friendly and easy to read.
