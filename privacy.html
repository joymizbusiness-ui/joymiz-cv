Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Joymiz CV is built with privacy at its core. This policy explains how we handle your data - and the short answer is: we don't.

Data Collection

We collect absolutely no personal data.

Joymiz CV is a purely client-side application. All data you enter into the CV builder - your name, email, work history, education, skills, and any other information - stays entirely within your web browser. Nothing is transmitted to our servers or any third party.

How It Works

Joymiz CV runs entirely in your browser using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. There is:

  • No server-side processing of your CV data
  • No database storing your information
  • No API calls sending your data anywhere
  • No accounts or user profiles

When you close the browser tab or navigate away, your CV data is gone (unless you downloaded the PDF).

Cookies

Joymiz CV does not use cookies. Not analytics cookies, not tracking cookies, not any cookies.

Analytics & Tracking

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other analytics or tracking services. We have no interest in tracking your behavior.

Third-Party Services

Joymiz CV does not load any third-party scripts, fonts, or resources that could track you. The entire application is self-contained.

Contact Form

If you use the contact form on our website, the information you provide (name, email, message) is used solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not add you to mailing lists or share this information with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Joymiz CV is not directed at children under 13. Since we collect no data from anyone, this is largely a formality, but we take children's privacy seriously.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this privacy policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. We will never retroactively apply changes to data we never collected in the first place.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@joymiz.com.
